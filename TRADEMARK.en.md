# Trademark policy

[Tiếng Việt](TRADEMARK.md) | **English**

> **Draft; needs review by a lawyer.**

The Apache-2.0 license applies to the source code of Hosso Studio. It does **not** grant the right to use the names and logos "Hosso" and "Hosso Studio" (section 6 of Apache-2.0).

- A fork or redistribution must **change the name and logo** before release.
- You may truthfully state the origin, for example "based on Hosso Studio".
- You may not use the name or logo in a way that suggests the product is released or endorsed by HOSSO.
- The product name, logo and brand color live in a single configuration block in `apps/studio/hosso.config.ts`, so forks can replace them easily.
